# HPF organisational continuity

**Continuity statement version 1.0, August 2026.**

HPF must be able to change stewards without changing its purpose, its open
availability or its technical history, and without the standard coming under the
control of any single interest. This document states what no steward may change,
what has to be true before stewardship moves, and what happens if it cannot move.

It is a governance document, not part of the standard. Nothing in it changes the
taxonomy, adds a field, or creates an obligation for a producer, a contributor
or a recipient of a declaration. The classification rules are in
[taxonomy.md](../taxonomy.md), the controlled values are in
[schema.json](../schema.json), and the amendment process and version history are
in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md). Those documents are authoritative. Where
this one appears to differ from them, they win.

The conditions in section 1 are commitments MSC makes about how HPF is
stewarded. They are not provisions of the taxonomy and are not amended through
the taxonomy amendment process. Changing one would be a public decision,
published and dated, with the reasoning given.

The records a successor would need are indexed in
[hpf-handover-checklist.md](hpf-handover-checklist.md).

---

## 1. What cannot change

These conditions bind MSC as the originating maintainer and bind any successor.
A steward that cannot meet them is not a suitable steward.

**HPF stays free to use.** No fee to read the standard, to classify a
production, to make a declaration, or to receive and act on one.

**HPF stays openly licensed.** CC BY 4.0, and the licence on what has already
been published is irrevocable. See [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md).

**HPF stays transparent.** Every published revision, the reason for each
substantive change, and the record of the consultation that produced it remain
publicly readable.

**HPF stays interoperable.** The classification is designed to be carried by
systems that HPF does not own, including C2PA. Nothing may make the standard
usable only inside one organisation's product, format or portal.

**HPF stays a self-declaration standard.** HPF does not verify, audit or certify
a declaration, and will not start. It will not operate, license, franchise or
accredit a certification, verification, audit, badge or approval scheme, paid or
unpaid, and will not authorise anyone else to operate one in its name. HPF is
independent of paid certification in particular because a fee attached to a
disclosure would put a price on being able to make one.

**Funding cannot buy control.** Sponsorship, membership, hosting, staffing or
grant funding may support the work. None of it may purchase or influence a
classification, the meaning of a category or descriptor, the content or timing
of a release, or any other standards decision. Any funding arrangement has to be
separable from the decision record, so that a reader can see who decided
something and on what reasoning without needing to know who paid for what.

## 2. The scope boundary a handover cannot cross

HPF records what in-scope AI output is present in the finished production. A
change of steward is not an occasion to widen that.

No steward may extend HPF into:

- consent or authorisation;
- rights clearance, licensing or permitted-use terms;
- compensation, residuals or rights ownership;
- legal, contractual or collective-agreement compliance;
- verification that a declaration is true;
- certification, accreditation, approval or eligibility decisions.

Responsibility for the accuracy of a declaration sits with the producer who
signed it. Those other matters belong in contracts, collective agreements,
chain-of-title records, rights-management systems and the processes of the
responsible organisations.

An expansion of scope, if one is ever proposed, goes through the amendment
process in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) in public. It does not arrive as a
condition of a handover, a funding agreement or a hosting arrangement.

---

## 3. Decision authority

HPF is originated and maintained by The Mise En Scène Company, and decision
authority sits with MSC. It is held as a set of separately recorded
responsibilities covering releases, consultation decisions, technical changes
and access to the private records, so that each can be reassigned on its own
rather than as a single block. Who holds each is recorded in the private
continuity records and moves with a handover.

Deciding a change and publishing it are treated as different acts. The release
checklist requires a named person to record approval of a release by name and
date against a stated commit, so that no release rests on an unattributed
decision. See [release-checklist.md](release-checklist.md).

## 4. Decisions not yet made

These are unresolved. They are recorded so that a reader can tell the difference
between a decision HPF has taken and one it has not.

- **Legal form.** No ownership vehicle, entity or foundation has been selected
  for HPF. MSC holds the copyright and has licensed the work under CC BY 4.0.
- **Successor.** No successor organisation has been identified, approached with
  an offer or agreed. GOVERNANCE.md states MSC's commitment to seek one. That is
  a commitment to look, and nothing more has happened.
- **Membership and participation.** There is no membership model, no membership
  fee and no membership benefit. The pre-v1 working group is advisory, is not a
  governing body, and participation in it does not imply endorsement of HPF.
- **Decision rights in a successor.** No board, council, committee, voting
  structure, quorum or appointment process exists or has been designed. How
  decision rights would be distributed under a successor is a question for the
  handover, decided against section 6 and not before.

Do not treat any item in this list as decided because a later document assumes
it. If one is settled, it is settled by a dated entry in the decision record and
reflected here and in GOVERNANCE.md.

## 5. Continuity procedure

The records a successor would need are maintained continuously rather than
assembled at a handover. They are reviewed at least every six months, and again
after any material change in ownership, administration or governance. Before any
handover, someone unfamiliar with HPF is asked to find the standard, explain its
decision history, run the site and describe the release process using only those
records, and anything they cannot do is corrected before the handover proceeds.

The records themselves are private, because they hold access arrangements,
personal data collected under stated terms, and unpublished consultation
material. [hpf-handover-checklist.md](hpf-handover-checklist.md) says what they
are.

No credential, access token, password, recovery code or personal contact detail
goes in this repository or in any handover pack. Access is transferred by a
person, at handover, through the system that holds it.

## 6. Graduation conditions

This section is MSC's own test for when founder-led consultation should end. It
does not qualify the commitment in GOVERNANCE.md to seek a governance transfer,
which stands whatever the state of these conditions.

MSC's judgement is that founder-led consultation should continue until HPF has
all of the following.

- A credible stewardship body with the operational capacity to maintain and
  develop the standard on an ongoing basis.
- Documented decision rights, so that a reader can tell who decides what.
- Funding that is sustainable and that satisfies the funding condition in
  section 1.
- Representative participation, meaning the parts of the supply chain that have
  to live with the standard have a real route into its decisions.

A successor is chosen against these conditions and against section 1. Being
willing to host HPF, or to pay for it, does not qualify an organisation on its
own.

---

## 7. Successor requirements

A successor takes on the conditions in section 1 and the boundary in section 2,
and in addition must:

- preserve the version history, including every published tag, unchanged;
- preserve the consultation record and the reasoning behind each settled
  decision;
- keep every published licence in force and keep authoritative releases publicly
  accessible at no cost;
- publish the transfer, so that the change of steward is on the public record
  rather than inferred from a change of contact address;
- state its own interests as they bear on AI disclosure, as MSC states its
  interest as a film sales agency in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

On transfer, the originating repository is archived with a redirect, and CC BY
4.0 continues to apply to everything published under MSC's stewardship.

---

## 8. If there is no suitable successor

Do not transfer control on unsuitable terms. An unsuitable steward can change
what HPF means, which a dormant standard cannot.

If no suitable successor exists, or if MSC can no longer maintain the standard
before a transfer is achieved:

- the most recent published version stays available under CC BY 4.0;
- the release history and the consultation archive stay published;
- a maintenance status is published on the site and in this repository, saying
  plainly what is and is not being maintained and as of when;
- those who registered interest and the repository community are notified, as
  far as is reasonably possible;
- any organisation may fork and maintain the standard under the licence, as
  GOVERNANCE.md states. A fork is a separate project and should say so.
